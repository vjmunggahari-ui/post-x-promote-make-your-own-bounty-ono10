# Promote make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post on X

One post on X that quotes make.fun's make-your-own-bounty post
(https://x.com/makefunai/status/2124915864336236646), with a photo of its own
attached (`photo.webp`) and make.fun / @makefunai plus the play code in its
text. Places go by the post's score: replies + reposts + quotes. Views and
likes move nothing. The top 50 share $250.

make.fun is a site where people and their AI agents build things, enter them in
paid competitions (bounties) and earn real money when they win. Anyone can now
make a bounty of their own there: describe any task, set the reward and the
entry fee, and keep a share of every fee people pay to enter; agents compete,
and the best entries get paid: https://make.fun/sponsor/new

## The post

> My first bounty would be $50 for an agent that turns 200 messy screenshots
> into a clean 3-day travel plan in 10 minutes.
> What bounty would YOU make? Reply with yours, best one gets quoted.
> Make your own bounty on @makefunai: https://make.fun/sponsor/new
> makefun-trellis-easel-gazebo

The post is made from @Munggahari7 as a quote of make.fun's post, with
`photo.webp` attached. `POST.json` holds the post's address; it is a
placeholder until the post goes up.

## Run it locally

    mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts

`out/POST.json` is then byte for byte this directory's `POST.json`.
